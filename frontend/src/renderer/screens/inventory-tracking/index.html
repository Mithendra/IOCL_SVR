1. Current Stock Levels
ItemUnitOpening (editable)Received (Today) Sold (Today)ClosingReorder LevelStatus

Opening is the tracked stock level and can be typed over directly by a Manager or Owner — it replaces the stored figure rather than adding to it, so use it to set the initial count or correct a miscount. Entering 0 is allowed and sets the item to zero. Use Restock Entry below instead when goods actually arrive — that adds to stock and keeps a dated receipt trail.

Closing Stock = Opening + Received − Sold. Sold (Today) is a live preview from that day's Daily Sales Entry rows; the stock decrement is applied at Daily Trial Balance finalization. Status shows "Low" once Closing falls to or below the Reorder Level.
